Baarle-Nassau: The Town Where the Border Runs Through Houses

Belgium / Netherlands

The story

One town, two countries, and a patchwork of tangled pieces. Dozens of Belgian and Dutch enclaves are stitched together so tightly that the border runs between houses, and in a few cases through them.

Why it matters

Many doors carry two house numbers, and the one that counts is the one on the door you walk through. White crosses and lines on the pavement mark the frontier.

Visitor tip

Start at the tourist office and follow the line on foot or by bike. It is flat, quiet and easy to combine with a day in nearby Breda or Tilburg.
