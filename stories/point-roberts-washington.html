Point Roberts: The American Town You Can Only Drive To Through Canada

United States

The story

A small piece of the United States on a peninsula south of Vancouver. By land you can only reach it by driving through Canada, so locals cross an international border for school, shopping and work.

Why it matters

It sits just south of the 49th parallel, which is how it ended up American.

Visitor tip

Bring passports for everyone and check border wait times before you go. Summer weekends can be busy.
