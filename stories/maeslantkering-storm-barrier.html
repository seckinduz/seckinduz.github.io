Maeslantkering: The Giant Storm Barrier That Floats Shut

Netherlands

The story

A giant storm surge barrier near Rotterdam, finished in 1997 as part of the Delta Works. Its two 210-metre arms stay in dry docks and float out to seal the waterway when a major storm surge is expected.

Why it matters

The visitor centre explains how it works. A planned test closing is a spectacle if you can time it.
