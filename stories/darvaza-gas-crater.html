Darvaza Gas Crater: Inside the Door to Hell

Turkmenistan

The story

A burning crater in the Karakum Desert, nicknamed the Door to Hell. It has been on fire for decades after a gas field collapsed.

Why it matters

Go at night when the fire is most dramatic. Stay behind the safe distance and follow your guide.
