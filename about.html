About Weirdest Borders & Hidden Places

Weirdest Borders & Hidden Places is a small independent geography and curiosity project. We make short videos and articles about enclaves, exclaves, odd borders and remote places that make people look at the map twice.

What we publish

Our content appears on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and this website gathers it in one place: an interactive world map of 25 weird borders and hidden places, in-depth articles for each location, short Web Stories and a geography quiz.

How we research

Our articles are written from widely available public information. We aim to describe the main historical and geographic facts clearly and to say when a detail is approximate. Coordinates are approximate, and rules about travel and access can change, so please check official sources before planning a trip. If you notice a mistake, tell us and we will review it.

Our e-book

Our 25 Weirdest Borders E-Book collects the stories, visitor tips and packing ideas for all 25 places. It is available in English and Spanish: English edition and Spanish edition.
