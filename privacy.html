Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details on this site. Our server logs and hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the time of the visit. We use this kind of information only to keep the site secure and to understand how it is used.

Local storage

The site may store your language choice (English or Spanish) in your browser's local storage so that it can remember your preference. This information stays on your device and can be deleted in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Maps: the interactive map uses Leaflet and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. When the map loads, your browser connects to these services, which may receive your IP address and technical details. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Code libraries: some scripts and styles are loaded from public content delivery networks, such as unpkg.
  • External links: links to Google Maps, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Beacons and Gumroad lead to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. Purchases of our e-book are handled by Gumroad.

Advertising and cookies

We may display ads from third-party vendors, including Google, to help support the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we collect very little personal data, most requests can be handled by clearing your browser data or by contacting us through the channels on our contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the options on our contact page.
