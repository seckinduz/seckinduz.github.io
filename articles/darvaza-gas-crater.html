The story in short

A burning crater in the Karakum Desert, nicknamed the Door to Hell. It has been on fire for decades after a gas field collapsed. Places like this show how borders are drawn by treaties, wars, surveys and geography, and why a line on a map can shape daily life long after the original decision.

How to visit

Go at night when the fire is most dramatic. Stay behind the safe distance and follow your guide.
