Utrecht Centraal: Inside One of the World's Biggest Bicycle Garages

Resumen en español: Uno de los mayores aparcamientos de bicicletas del mundo, abierto en 2018 con espacio para 12.500 bicis. Es un buen lugar para entender por qué los Países Bajos giran en torno a la bicicleta. Consejo: Llega en tren: el aparcamiento esta justo bajo la estacion. Después recorre los canales antiguos.

Under and around the main railway station in Utrecht lies one of the largest bicycle parking facilities in the world. Opened in 2018, it offers room for about 12,500 bicycles. At first glance it looks like a modern underground car park, but instead of cars it is filled with rows of neatly stacked bikes, bright signs and cyclists gliding through lanes.

It is a good place to understand why the Netherlands is often described as a country built around the bicycle.

Country / regionNetherlands

Why so many bikes?

Cycling is a daily habit in Dutch cities. People ride to school, work, shops and the train station, and in many towns bikes outnumber cars on the street. Railway stations are natural hubs because commuters combine a bike ride with a train journey, and they need a safe place to leave their bike for the day.

In Utrecht the number of cyclists grew so quickly that older parking areas were overwhelmed. Bikes were piled along the sidewalks and in front of the station entrances. The city decided to build a high-capacity facility as part of a major redevelopment of the station area.

How the garage works

The parking area is designed to be easy to use. Cyclists ride down gentle ramps, find a free space with the help of digital signs and park their bikes in double-decker racks. An electronic system tells riders where there are open places. The first twenty-four hours are free, which makes it convenient for travelers.

The garage is also connected directly with the station concourse, so people can park and walk to their train in a few minutes. Staff and technology help keep the area organized, and there are services for repairs, cleaning and rentals.

What to see and do

Arrive by train and walk down to the garage. Visitors can look around, take pictures of the endless rows of bikes and see how locals use the space. Afterwards, explore the old city of Utrecht, with its canals, medieval towers and sunken wharf cellars.

If you want to ride yourself, rent a bike and follow the cycle paths. Bring a sturdy lock, a rain poncho and a small basket or bag. Keep to the right, signal when turning and remember that cyclists have a lot of priority in the Netherlands.

A few numbers and practical tips

The numbers behind the Utrecht garage help explain its scale. It offers space for roughly twelve and a half thousand bikes, spread over several levels, and it is one part of a large redevelopment that also reshaped the station hall and the surrounding streets. To use it, arrive with a bicycle or visit simply to look around. If you plan to travel by train, remember to note the level and row where you parked, because the rows can look very similar. Rental bikes are available nearby, and they are a good way to explore the city's canals and parks.

Why it matters

The Utrecht bicycle garage shows that infrastructure can encourage healthy and sustainable habits. By giving cyclists safe, convenient parking, the city makes cycling an easy choice. It is a practical example that other cities study when they want to reduce traffic and improve quality of life. For travelers, it is also a surprisingly entertaining sight, a cathedral of bicycles in the middle of a station.
