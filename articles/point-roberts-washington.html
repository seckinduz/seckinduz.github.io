Point Roberts: The American Town You Can Only Drive To Through Canada

A small piece of the United States on a peninsula south of Vancouver. By land you can only reach it by driving through Canada, so locals cross an international border for school, shopping and work. It sits just south of the 49th parallel, which is how it ended up American.

The story in short

A small piece of the United States on a peninsula south of Vancouver. By land you can only reach it by driving through Canada, so locals cross an international border for school, shopping and work. It sits just south of the 49th parallel, which is how it ended up American. Places like this show how borders are drawn by treaties, wars, surveys and geography, and why a line on a map can shape daily life long after the original decision.

How to visit

Bring passports for everyone and check border wait times before you go. Summer weekends can be busy.

What to pack

passports, a paper road map, a picnic blanket.
