The story in short

One town, two countries, and a patchwork of tangled pieces. Dozens of Belgian and Dutch enclaves are stitched together so tightly that the border runs between houses, and in a few cases through them. Many doors carry two house numbers, and the one that counts is the one on the door you walk through. White crosses and lines on the pavement mark the frontier. Places like this show how borders are drawn by treaties, wars, surveys and geography, and why a line on a map can shape daily life long after the original decision.

How to visit

Start at the tourist office and follow the line on foot or by bike. It is flat, quiet and easy to combine with a day in nearby Breda or Tilburg.

What to pack

comfortable walking shoes, a city bike lock, a small camera.
