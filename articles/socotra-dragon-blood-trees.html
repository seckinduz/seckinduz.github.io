Socotra: The Alien Island of Dragon's Blood Trees

An island so isolated that about a third of its plants grow nowhere else. Its icon is the dragon's blood tree, shaped like an umbrella and named for its dark red resin.

PlaceSocotra
Country / regionYemen
Coordinates (approx.)12.4634°N, 53.8237°E

The story in short

An island so isolated that about a third of its plants grow nowhere else. Its icon is the dragon's blood tree, shaped like an umbrella and named for its dark red resin. Places like this show how borders are drawn by treaties, wars, surveys and geography, and why a line on a map can shape daily life long after the original decision.

How to visit

Access is limited and conditions change, so check flights and the security situation before planning.

What to pack

a field guide to plants, a wide-brim hat, sturdy sandals.
