Kinderdijk: 19 Windmills That Drained a Polder

Nineteen windmills built around 1740 to drain the low polder. Today modern pumping stations do the heavy work, and the mills are protected as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

PlaceKinderdijk
Country / regionNetherlands
Coordinates (approx.)51.8833°N, 4.6333°E

The story in short

Nineteen windmills built around 1740 to drain the low polder. Today modern pumping stations do the heavy work, and the mills are protected as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Places like this show how borders are drawn by treaties, wars, surveys and geography, and why a line on a map can shape daily life long after the original decision.

How to visit

Rent a bike and ride the dike paths. Sunrise and early evening are the quietest times.

What to pack

a light jacket, a camera, a refillable bottle.
