Maeslantkering: The Giant Storm Barrier That Floats Shut

A giant storm surge barrier near Rotterdam, finished in 1997 as part of the Delta Works. Its two 210-metre arms stay in dry docks and float out to seal the waterway when a major storm surge is expected.

PlaceMaeslantkering
Country / regionNetherlands
Coordinates (approx.)51.9544°N, 4.1665°E

The story in short

A giant storm surge barrier near Rotterdam, finished in 1997 as part of the Delta Works. Its two 210-metre arms stay in dry docks and float out to seal the waterway when a major storm surge is expected. Places like this show how borders are drawn by treaties, wars, surveys and geography, and why a line on a map can shape daily life long after the original decision.

How to visit

The visitor centre explains how it works. A planned test closing is a spectacle if you can time it.

What to pack

binoculars, a notebook, comfortable shoes.
